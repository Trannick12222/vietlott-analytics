Mathematical Engine & Formulas Specification 21 Formulas

Comprehensive theoretical foundation, scoring algorithms, and statistical mechanics implemented in this platform.

⚠️
Methodological Disclosure: Lottery balls drawn in Mega 6/45 and Power 6/55 are physically independent random events with uniform baseline probability \(P = 6 / N\). The formulas below quantify historical empirical trends, stochastic regime clustering, combinatorial fitness, and risk mitigation — they do not guarantee winning.
Section I: Core Probability & Bayesian Analytics
#01. Theoretical Combinatorial Probability Combinatorics

Exact baseline probability of any specific single number appearing in a single draw:

P(X = k) = C(N - 1, 5) / C(N, 6) = 6 / N

Yields 13.33% for Mega 6/45 (6/45) and 10.91% for Power 6/55 (6/55).

#02. Poisson Rare-Event Distribution Stochastic

Probability of a specific ball number appearing exactly \(k\) times over \(n\) consecutive draws:

P(X = k) = (λ^k · e^(-λ)) / k!, where λ = n · (6 / N)

Models variance and standard deviation of ball frequency over moving windows.

#03. Bayesian Conjugate Updating Bayesian

Beta-Binomial conjugate posterior estimation of ball appearance likelihood:

E[θ|x] = (α + successes) / (α + β + n), with prior α/(α+β) = 6/N

Prevents extreme empirical noise from small sample sizes via Bayesian regularization.

#04. Geometric Recency & Gaps Law Recency

Probability of a ball remaining dormant for exactly \(k\) draws before re-emerging:

P(Gap = k) = (1 - p)^k · p, where p = 6 / N

Expected gap \(E[Gap] = (1 - p)/p \approx 6.5\) (Mega) or \(8.17\) (Power).

#05. Exponential Decay Momentum Time-Series

Decaying historical frequency weighting giving priority to recent performance:

W_freq(k) = Σ (e^(-λ · t_i)), where t_i = draw_recency

λ = 0.05 yields half-life decay of ~14 draws, filtering stale historical patterns.

#06. Pair Co-occurrence & Jaccard Index Correlation

Quantifying joint draw frequency affinity between ball pairs \((i, j)\):

J(i, j) = |D_i ∩ D_j| / |D_i ∪ D_j| = N_ij / (N_i + N_j - N_ij)

Detects affinity clusters and negative repelling tendencies across the matrix.

Section II: Ensemble Scoring & Structural Harmony Filters
#07. 8-Factor Ensemble Model Scoring Ensemble

Weighted multi-criteria composite score mapped onto 0–100 scale:

Score(k) = Σ w_i · Norm(S_i(k)), with Σ w_i = 1.0

Synthesizes frequency, recency, Bayes, co-occurrence, regime reversion & Monte Carlo.

#08. Central Limit Gaussian Sum Filter Distribution

Combinatorial sum bell curve expectation based on CLT:

μ_sum = 6(N + 1)/2; Fitness_sum = exp(-0.5 · ((S - μ)/σ)^2) · 100

6/45: μ = 138 (σ = 33.7); 6/55: μ = 168 (σ = 44.5). Rejects tickets in the tails.

#09. Natural Delta Steps Dispersion Delta Steps

Spacing between sorted numbers \(\Delta_i = x_{i+1} - x_i\):

E[Δ_avg] = N / 6; Fitness_Δ = max(0, 100 - |Δ̄ - N/6|·8 - Penalties)

Penalizes clumping (\(\ge 2\) consecutive triples) and unnatural gaps (\(> N/3\)).

#10. Hypergeometric Parity (Odd/Even) Filter Parity

Exact combinatorial probability of drawing \(k\) odd numbers:

P(Odd = k) = [C(N_odd, k) · C(N_even, 6 - k)] / C(N, 6)

3O:3E (~33%), 4O:2E (~24%), 2O:4E (~24%). Eliminates extreme 6O:0E / 0O:6E.

#11. Trailing Digit Diversity Modular

Uniform distribution of final digits \(d_i = x_i \pmod{10}\):

Diversity_digit = |Unique({x_i mod 10})| ≥ 5

Penalizes ticket sets sharing 3 or more identical trailing digits (e.g. 03, 13, 23, 33).

#12. Shannon Spatial Entropy Information

Information entropy across 5 numerical buckets \([B_1, \dots, B_5]\):

H = - Σ (p_i · log_2(p_i)), where p_i = count(B_i) / 6

Maximized entropy ensures wide coverage across all decimal tiers (1–9, 10–19, etc.).

Section III: Extreme Value Theory, Markov Regimes & Repeat Constraints
#13. Order Statistics & Extreme Value Bounds EVT

Expected values of minimal \(X_{(1)}\) and maximal \(X_{(6)}\) numbers:

E[X_(1)] = (N + 1) / 7; E[X_(6)] = 6 · (N + 1) / 7

Mega 6/45: \(E[X_1] \approx 6.6\), \(E[X_6] \approx 39.4\); Power 6/55: \(E[X_1] \approx 8.0\), \(E[X_6] \approx 48.0\).

#14. Combinatorial Span (Spread) Order Stats

Theoretical difference between largest and smallest ball drawn:

Span = X_(6) - X_(1); E[Span] = 5 · (N + 1) / 7

Optimal span is 32.8 in Mega and 40.0 in Power. Penalizes compressed spreads (\(< 20\)).

#15. Markov Regime Switching Matrix Markov Chain

Transition probabilities between Low Compression, Bell Curve, and High Extreme:

P(S_(t+1) = j | S_t = i) = N_ij / Σ_k N_ik

Dynamically forecasts whether the upcoming draw will contract low or expand high.

#16. Mean Reversion Oscillator Oscillator

Normalized Z-score measuring statistical overbought / oversold regimes:

Z_rev = (X̄_recent - μ_sum) / (σ_sum / √k)

Detects elastic pullback after extreme draws (e.g. following low-compressed #1409).

#17. Repeat Constraint (Historical Filter) Hard Filter

Eliminating excessive carry-over balls from immediately preceding draw:

|Combo ∩ LastDraw| ≤ 2 (Heavy penalty -60 if ≥ 3)

Empirical validation: 0.0% of historical draws repeated ≥ 3 numbers. Eliminates dead-weight balls.

#18. Consecutive Ball Clumping Penalty Combinatorics

Penalizing sequential number runs \(x, x+1, x+2\):

Penalty_run = 20.0 · max(0, Count(Δ = 1) - 1)

Allows at most 1 pair of adjacent numbers (e.g. 23–24); forbids 3+ sequential balls.

Section IV: Combinatorial Wheeling Systems & Optimization
#19. Covering Design C(v, k, t, m) Steiner Systems

Guarantees a match of \(t\) numbers if \(m\) numbers hit within pool \(v\):

Covering(v=10, k=6, t=3, m=3) = 4 tickets

Compresses 210 full permutations into 4 tickets, saving 98.1% of capital.

#20. Triplet Subspace Coverage Metric Coverage

Ratio of covered 3-element subsets over all \(\binom{v}{3}\) possible triplets:

Coverage = |U_t (Triplets(t_i))| / C(v, 3) · 100%

Quantifies mathematical guarantee of winning 3rd-tier prize packages.

#21. Cross-Matrix Suitability Index Decision Theory

Relative utility score advising whether to play Mega 6/45 or Power 6/55:

Score_type = Σ (w_j · Fitness_j(numbers, Config_type))

Evaluates span compactness, boundary ceiling (\(\le 45\)), and jackpot prize expected value.
